Live dashboards, automatic lap detection, corner-by-corner comparison, 3D visualizations, and a community tune library.

Compare lap 2 vs my personal best. Where am I losing time?
Three losses. T3 late apex (+0.089s): you brake 14m later and carry 6 km/h extra into apex, but throttle stays flat for +0.42s past the clipping point. Try an earlier apex and throttle-on before the curb.
And the tires?
Front-left core hit 103°C on lap 3, above the grip-drop cliff. Drop front pressures −0.4 psi or reduce toe-out by 0.1° to pull peak temps back.

Local-first

No subscription. No cloud.

Every lap you record stays in a local SQLite database at %APPDATA%/raceiq. The only thing that leaves your machine is the prompt you explicitly send to your AI provider.

  • 01
    UDP loopback on 127.0.0.1:5301
    Lossless sub-ms capture — even while forwarding to a wheelbase.
  • 02
    Shared memory for ACC
    Richer data than UDP alone — pad wear, damage state, brake bias.
  • 03
    BYO LLM
    Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Groq — or run Ollama on the same PC.
